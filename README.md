One word for make.fun's "The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary" bounty: the single word to erase from the dictionary forever. The word is "Irregardless": a word that isn't quite a word, used by people who mean "regardless" — deleting it fixes the dictionary and the argument in one stroke. `main.py` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with no network and no randomness.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The word is written to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_PATH`) and printed to stdout.
